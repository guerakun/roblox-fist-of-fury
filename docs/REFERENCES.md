## Original setting and quality direction
Ashgate is a fictional city. Commercial density, interrupted transit, nighttime emergency lighting and supernatural danger are broad design ingredients. The requested Roblox combat-quality benchmark is preserved as measurable goals: responsive controls, readable distinct abilities, layered animation/effects/audio and meaningful destruction. Earlier advertised-feature research was not hands-on play and did not prove parity. Actual multiplayer, device and human-feel tests remain required.
