# Curtain Break — story and experience record

## Active campaign: Dead Signal
Three directed chapters carry the squad through **Ashgate Crossing → abandoned station → abandoned factory**. Every chapter has a miniboss and boss. This is original fiction; the mission and setting do not recreate an existing story.

An emergency broadcast repeats above Ashgate's deserted crossing. A supernatural curtain is drawing power through the abandoned transit line into Kurogane Works. The squad must reopen the crossing, trace the signal through the station and destroy the furnace relay so evacuation can resume. Ordinary commercial signs become transit warnings, then industrial lockout notices: the scenery explains the route.

## Three chapters, six confrontations
### 01 — Ashgate Crossing / Open the cordon
Commercial signs glow above an empty response van and failed traffic signal. Crosswalk Executioner guards the crossing; Siren Marshal holds the evacuation cordon farther down the street. Their defeat opens the route to Exit 13, an original transit marker in this fictional city. Road stripes make forward movement readable. The first boss should reward noticing warnings and punishing recovery.

### 02 — Abandoned station / Follow the last departure
The train is still here; the passengers are not. Platform Widow patrols beneath a stopped clock. Last Conductor stands between a derailed carriage and a departure signal that never changed. A maintenance board points to the industrial line. The station is the middle chapter, not total victory.

### 03 — Abandoned factory / Silence the source
A stopped conveyor feeds the furnace hall. Furnace Hound guards the assembly line; Kiln Sovereign waits before Furnace 03's barred core. Larger machines and amber work lights provide culmination while attack-warning colors stay consistent. Breaking the relay makes evacuation possible. Current victory UI is distinct from the unimplemented train escape or civilian procession.
