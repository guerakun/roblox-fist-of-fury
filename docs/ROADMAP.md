# Production roadmap

## Current slice acceptance
Startup without runtime errors; playable three-stage route; percent changes only on valid hit; launch strength increases with percent; stock loss/respawn; all-stock party wipe; retry; gate/rally progression; boss and victory; hero switching without healing exploit; real Toolbox assets present.

## Quality target: Jujutsu Shenanigans
Reference responsiveness, readable ability identity, animation/VFX/sound layering and satisfying destruction. The prototype is not at parity. Required production passes:
1. Two-to-four-client tests under 100–200ms latency and disconnect/late-join cases.
2. Authored ability choreography (Rasengan-like spiral contact, elastic stretch, directional water arc); aerial chains and enemy hit-react/ragdoll recovery.
3. Environment destruction using tagged authored prop assemblies, bounded debris and deterministic resets.
4. Mesh/texture pass for character faces, hair and clothing; curse enemy silhouettes and boss multi-phase attacks.
5. Combat audio mix, music commissioned/licensed for the game, environment ambience.
6. Mobile/tablet/controller usability sessions and measured frame-time budgets.
7. Full authored chapter dialogue, rescue interactions, checkpoint cinematics and progression rewards.
8. Publish owned animations and verify every asset in the final universe; permission/credit audit.
9. Accessibility: effect intensity slider, camera shake toggle, color-independent warnings.
10. Save progression, matchmaking/lobby, challenge modifiers and completion rewards after core feel is proven.

## Preserved future ideas
Station basement, train platform siege, rooftop curtain anchors, blackout crossings, clone-assisted rescue, team launch finishers, water-flow crowd control, elastic ally rescue, optional timed survivor routes, elite curse mutations and replay score medals. These are backlog ideas, not claims of shipped systems.
